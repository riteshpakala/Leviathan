Mador Halfell

Among the luthiers of Veldmar, Mador Halfell is remembered for patient, exacting work. Letters place the birth of Mador Halfell in 1746. The childhood home of Mador Halfell stood in Pinebrook. Travellers who passed through Veldmar wrote often about Mador Halfell.

Mador Halfell learned the trade from Lomon Evmark, a demanding teacher. Much of the work of Mador Halfell survives in the lower rooms of the archive. The story of Mador Halfell is still told at the autumn fairs. Some of the details about Mador Halfell are disputed in the older chronicles.
